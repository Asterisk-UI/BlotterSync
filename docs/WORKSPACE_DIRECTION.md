# Workspace direction

Mode: Operate. Code-first extension of the existing slate-and-blue BlotterSync officer interface, preserving its product identity and legacy routes. No new visual world or marketing claims.

## First viewport
The incident overview shows the current officer's record totals and register, with a desk assistant alongside. The mechanism is selecting a real record, preparing a source-linked brief and recording a deliberate review decision. Primary action: New incident. Demo records are explicitly synthetic and local extraction is distinct from model output.

## Interaction and responsive contract
The assistant panel changes from guidance to incident detail, source-linked draft and review actions. Intake begins with original notes, then transfers an accepted draft into an explicit filing form. Source text and concise tool activity remain inspectable. At mobile widths the sidebar collapses to navigation and the panels stack without document overflow. Forms remain native, labeled and keyboard-accessible. Error, loading, empty, review and disabled states use the same component vocabulary.

## Quality bar
A desk officer should find a pending incident, identify the source, distinguish local from model assistance and understand whether a draft was accepted or filed without learning new terminology. The interface preserves the original Segoe UI/system font, blue actions and slate navigation while improving spacing and table hierarchy. No decorative imagery is needed for this records task.
